XianyuScheduler V4.15

- Adaptive pacing: fast actions move faster; time-sensitive tasks keep required dwell time.
- Reuses freshly verified TASK_PANEL OCR during completion verification.
- Reduces completion verification from 4 fixed OCR checks to 2-3 adaptive checks.
- Shortens post-task handoff delays and varies them within bounded ranges.
- Replaces fixed 8.5 s bounce wait with task-category-specific timing.
- Preserves explicit 15s/30s task duration requirements.
- Prioritizes learned edge-back recovery before force-opening Xianyu.
- Exits persistent Android system-transition foreground early instead of waiting the full task timer.
- Defers diagnostic screenshots until repeated failure/unverified states.
- Manual takeover final status is ABORTED instead of FINISHED.
- V4.13 learning data and V4.14 learned-return behavior remain compatible.
